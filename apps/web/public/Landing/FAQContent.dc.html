FAQ
Questions, answered.
Getting started
Do I need special hardware, or does a phone camera work?{{i1}}
Just the phone already in your crew's pocket. The in-app camera adds grid lines, level guidance, and before/after and scan modes on top of a normal phone camera — no extra equipment to buy or carry.
How long does it take to get a crew up and running?{{i2}}
Most teams are capturing photos into their first project within minutes of signing up. There's no migration step — you start documenting new work immediately and import older photos as you go.
Does it work without cell signal on site?{{i3}}
Yes. Capture works fully offline — photos, video, and walkthroughs queue on the device and sync automatically once you're back in range. Nothing is lost in a dead zone.
Using Everlumen
Can clients see progress without downloading an app?{{i4}}
Yes. Share a link to a curated gallery or a generated report and it opens in any browser — no account, login, or app install required on their end.
How accurate are the AI-drafted reports — do I still need to check them?{{i5}}
The draft pulls directly from your timestamped photos, GPS data, and notes — it's grounded in what was actually captured, not guesswork. You always review and can edit before sending; most teams find a quick read-through takes under a minute versus the hour it used to take to write from scratch.
Can I control who sees what — subs, clients, and my own office staff?{{i6}}
Yes, on Team plans. Give clients a curated gallery, subs their own scope of work, and admins the full picture — all from one set of role-based permissions you control.
Security & data
Who owns the photos and data — can I export everything if I leave?{{i7}}
You own everything you capture. Full project exports — photos, reports, and walkthroughs — are available any time from account settings, and stay available for a period after cancellation.
Is a timestamped photo actually reliable enough to settle a dispute?{{i8}}
Every photo carries its original capture time and GPS location, set automatically and not editable after the fact — that's what makes it a record your team can point to with confidence, not just a photo.
Billing & plans
What happens if I need to cancel?{{i9}}
Cancel any time from account settings — no phone call or contract required. Your data stays exportable for a period afterward so you're never locked out of your own job record.
Is there a free trial, and does it need a credit card?{{i10}}
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You'll only be asked for billing details if you decide to continue.
