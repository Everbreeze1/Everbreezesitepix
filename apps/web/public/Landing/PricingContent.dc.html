Billing questions
Can I switch plans later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time from account settings; changes apply on your next billing cycle.
What counts as a "user"?
Anyone with a login — office staff and field crew both count. Subcontractors given scoped project access do not count against your seat total on Team.
Is there a contract?
No. Month-to-month, cancel any time — annual billing is available if you'd rather lock in a rate.
