Getting started
Setting up your first blueprint
A blueprint bundles the checklists, workflow phases, document templates and report templates a job type always needs. Go to Blueprints → New blueprint, then attach checklists from your library and lay out the phases in the order your crew actually works them. Once saved, assigning that blueprint to a project pre-fills all of it automatically.
Inviting your crew and setting roles
Under Teams → Crew, use "Invite crew member" to send an email invite. Owner and Manager roles can see every project; Standard crew only see the ones they're assigned to. Check Roles & Permissions to see exactly what each role can and can't do before inviting someone.
Adding a subcontractor to a project
Subcontractors live on Teams → Subcontractors, separate from your W-2 crew. Add their company, trade, primary contact and insurance expiration there once, then assign them to any project the same way you'd assign an employee.
